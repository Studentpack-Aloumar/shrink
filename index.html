Shrink GNSS files for AUSPOS

Turns large RINEX files into small ones that upload fast. AUSPOS gives the same result. Your files never leave this computer.

  1. 1Add files
  2. 2Save
  3. 3Send to AUSPOS

Now send them to AUSPOS

Open AUSPOS ↗
  1. Click Choose File(s) and select all the small files from your Downloads folder.
  2. Check the heights match:
  1. If it says No valid antenna type, leave DEFAULT(NONE).
  2. Enter the email for the results and click Submit. A green box with a job number means it worked.
What does this do?
  • AUSPOS only uses GPS and samples every 30 seconds. This tool removes the other satellite systems and the extra samples before upload, so files are about 70 times smaller.
  • Antenna height, antenna type and station name are kept unchanged.
  • It checks each file has at least 1 hour of data, and that files sent together overlap by more than 1 hour.
  • Wait until after 03:00 UTC the day after measuring before submitting.
